MERIDIAN 59 PRIME - CLASSES AND GEAR (working template, edit freely)
====================================================================
Status 2026-10-22. Skills and levels are templates and will be tuned. Stats are not shown at character creation.

CLASSES AND STARTING STATS (might / stamina / agility / intellect / mysticism / aim)
  Warrior   50 / 50 / 50 /  1 /  1 / 50
  Monk      50 / 30 / 50 / 50 / 50 / 30   (the old Priest became the Monk; a Priest comes back later)
  Paladin   50 / 40 / 30 / 30 / 30 / 30   (tank that heals: less armor and damage than a warrior, less healing than a monk)

  Weapons: Monks use no weapons. Warriors and Paladins: no daggers, no ranged weapons.

COMBAT NOTES
  Defense = Parry + Block (+ shield bonus) + Dodge x2 (Monk: x3) + Agility x8 + a health term + gear defense.
  Fists (Punch stroke): base damage 2-11 (+5 and +25% at max skill and stats, about 9-20): already near the best melee weapons, so no monk damage buff.
  Touch spells use Punch (or 2 x Mysticism) as their proficiency.
